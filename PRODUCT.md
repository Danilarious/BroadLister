# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BroadLister is used by Bo and Sevenfold operators who are building sourced media lists, reviewing journalist/outlet/article records, and preparing client-specific campaign overlays. They are usually doing focused desktop work, but need the interface to remain readable and usable from a phone when checking status or reviewing a small queue remotely.

## Product Purpose

BroadLister is a local-first media intelligence and media-relationship operations workspace. It helps Sevenfold maintain a reusable global media graph while keeping client strategy, narrative fit, approvals, and relationship state isolated in client overlays. Success means operators can move from import or article URL to reviewed global records, campaign list, approval, and export without losing provenance or leaking one client’s private strategy into another client’s view.

## Brand Personality

Focused, careful, editorial. The tool should feel like a disciplined internal media desk: calm enough for sensitive client work, precise enough for source audits, and fast enough for daily list building.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a generic CRM, sales pipeline, mass outreach tool, spam dashboard, glassy AI demo, or decorative SaaS admin template. Avoid loud growth-product aesthetics, pipeline language, sequence/cadence metaphors, hidden automation cues, and anything that implies BroadLister sends messages.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve operator authority: every approval, export, verification, and relationship judgment must feel deliberate and human-controlled.
2. Keep global facts visually distinct from client overlays: reusable public intelligence and client-private strategy must not blur together.
3. Make provenance visible without making the interface heavy: source confidence should be easy to inspect while keeping primary workflows fast.
4. Reduce cognitive load through progressive disclosure: show enough to act, reveal complex JSON-backed fields through structured panels, not raw data dumps.
5. Design for quiet trust: clear hierarchy, restrained color, readable tables, explicit safety copy, and no decorative distractions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-reachable review actions, touch targets of at least 44px on mobile, reduced-motion support, readable text at narrow widths, and layouts that avoid horizontal scrolling on phones. Copy should be direct, specific, and free of em dashes.
